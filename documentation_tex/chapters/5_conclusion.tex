\section{Conclusion}
In the benchmarks performed for both algorithms, a clear trend was established. 
The simulated annealing algorithm performed better for both datasets, which indicates that the combined approach is not beneficial.
The crossover and selection showed to have influence on the search for a better tour, as for both datasets the best tour found was generated from a crossover with a consecutive mutation step.
Still, it was not enough to beat the annealing algorithm.
However, the insight on the behavior of a population and the establishment of a metric for the diversity still constitute a worthy endeavor.
The algorithm itself can be improved with better selection rules as well as a less destructive mutation for higher temperatures. 
Also the crossover part should be rethinked and could potentially benefit from different proposal probabilities based on useful genomes. 
The mean edge diversity could be used for the proposal of crossover pair indices.
There are many avenues that can be explored, but this is a task for another day.
